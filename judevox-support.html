JudeVox — Support

Pocket sampler and beat maker for iPhone and iPad. Totally free.

Getting started

Open JudeVox and press play: a demo beat is ready. Tap the ? button for the illustrated 12-page manual covering pads, knobs, sampling, keys mode, FX and songs.

Recording your voice

Tap SAMPLE, then hold any pad and make a sound. Let go to stop. If nothing records, check Settings › JudeVox › Microphone is on.

Turning the knobs

Put a finger on a knob and move it around in a circle, or twist with two fingers. Drag up or down from the centre for fine moves.

Your projects

JudeVox saves to 9 project slots automatically. Everything stays on your device; nothing is uploaded. See the privacy policy.
