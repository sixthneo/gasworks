Chimefall — Support

Draw a chime, let it fall. Calm, generative music for iPhone and iPad.

Getting started

Lift or tilt your device and the glowing orb starts to fall. Draw a line with your finger to make a chime in its path. Every time the orb touches a chime, it rings.

Notes and tuning

Longer chimes ring lower, shorter ones higher. Every chime snaps to the key and scale shown at the top left, so anything you draw sounds right together. Tap a chime to see its note and change its metal or thickness; drag either end of a straight chime to stretch it.

Controls

Groove nudges notes onto a slow beat. Perpetual keeps the orb bouncing forever. Hold freezes the orb. Undo removes the last chime (or two-finger tap). Double-tap to drop the orb where you touch. Long-press a chime to delete it.

Recording and sharing

Tap Record, let the orb play, then tap Stop. You can rename the recording and share it from the sheet that appears. All recordings are in Library, and in the Files app under On My iPhone › Chimefall.

Saving your chimes

Open Library › Saved chimes and tap Save what's on screen. Tap a saved arrangement to bring it back, or swipe to delete it.

iPad and the gravity dial

On iPad, or if tilting is awkward, turn on the on-screen gravity dial in Settings and drag its dot to point gravity.

No sound?

Turn up the volume. If another app took over audio, close and reopen Chimefall.

Privacy

Chimefall collects nothing. See the privacy policy.
