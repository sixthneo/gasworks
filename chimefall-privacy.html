Chimefall — Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

The short version

Chimefall does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

Motion

Chimefall reads your device's tilt to set the direction of gravity. This is used live on your device and never stored or sent anywhere.

Recordings and saved chimes

Recordings and saved arrangements are written to the app's folder on your device. They leave your device only if you choose to share them.

Network

Chimefall makes no network requests.

Children

Chimefall collects no information from anyone, including children.
