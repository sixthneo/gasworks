Gasworks G-1 — Support

A sixteen-voice fart synthesizer for iPhone and iPad. Totally free.

Playing

Tap any of the 16 pads to play a sound. Every sound is synthesized live, so each hit is slightly different.

Knobs

Put a finger on a knob and move it around in a circle, or twist with two fingers. You feel a click for each step.

Making a beat

Press play to start the 4-channel, 16-step sequencer. Tap steps to add or remove hits. Tap a channel to mute it, slide to change its level, long-press to choose its sound or solo it. Shuffle makes a random beat; Clear starts fresh.

No sound?

Turn up the volume and check the Volume knob. Gasworks plays even with the silent switch on.

Privacy

Gasworks collects nothing. See the privacy policy.
