JudeVox — Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

JudeVox is a sampler for iPhone and iPad. You record or import sounds and play them on pads. This policy covers the JudeVox app.

What we collect

Nothing. JudeVox has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it never sends data off your device.

Microphone and music

JudeVox asks for the microphone only so you can record samples, and only records while you hold a pad in sample mode. If you choose a song from your music library or a file, JudeVox reads just that audio to make a sample. Recordings and samples are saved inside the app on your device and are never uploaded.

What stays on your device

Your projects, patterns and samples are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app. You can copy them out yourself through the Files app.

TestFlight

If you test a beta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports, usage information and any feedback you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's privacy policy. We use that only to fix bugs.

Children

JudeVox collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
