Moodtape — Support

Pick a mood. Play music. A mood groovebox for iPhone and iPad.

Playing

Choose a mood and tap START. A band plays chords, bass and drums; tap any pad to play along. Every pad is in the song's key, so any order sounds right. Pads that light up belong to the current chord.

Mood pad

Drag the dot to move between moods and fine-tune the energy. The six keys below jump straight to a mood.

Knobs

Circle a finger around a knob or twist with two fingers. Tempo, Space (reverb), Tone and Mix (how loud the band is).

Lift me up

In Sad, tap Lift me up to let the music move from rainy minor to sunny major over 4, 8 or 12 minutes. Stay here keeps it soft.

Humming

Tap HUM, allow the microphone, then hold the red key and hum. Moodtape snaps your voice to the mood's key and puts it on a pad. If you said no to the microphone, turn it on in Settings › Moodtape.

Saving

Tap SAVE to start recording what you play and tap it again to stop. Share the audio file from the sheet that appears. Saved files are also in the Files app under Moodtape.

No sound?

Turn up the volume and check the Mix knob. Moodtape plays even with the silent switch on.

Privacy

Moodtape collects nothing. See the privacy policy.
