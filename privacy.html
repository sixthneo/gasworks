Gasworks G-1 — Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Gasworks G-1 is a sound toy: a synthesizer that makes its noises on your device. This policy covers the iPhone and iPad app and the web version.

What we collect

Nothing. Gasworks has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos, and it never sends data off your device.

What stays on your device

The app remembers your last knob and mixer settings so they are there next time you open it. That is stored only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

TestFlight

If you test a beta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports, usage information and any feedback you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's privacy policy. We use that only to fix bugs.

Children

Gasworks collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/sixthneo/gasworks.
