Moodtape — Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

The short version

Moodtape does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

Microphone

Moodtape uses the microphone only while you hold the hum key. The recording is analysed on your device to find its pitch and is kept only in the app's memory, or on your device if you write it to a pad. It is never uploaded.

Recordings you save

Audio you save is written to the app's folder on your device. It leaves your device only if you choose to share it.

Network

Moodtape makes no network requests.

Children

Moodtape collects no information from anyone, including children.
